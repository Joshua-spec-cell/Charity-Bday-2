Chapter One

A Letter to Charity

To Lady Charity,

If words could capture the entirety of a person, I suspect even the finest writers would find themselves short of words when writing about thee.

Thou art a lovely blend of kindness, intelligence, humour and warmth. There is something rather special about the way thou carriest thyself — the way thou carest, the way thou listenest, and the way thou makest even the simplest conversations worth looking forward to.

I admire thy love for learning, thy passion for language and literature, and the dedication thou bringest to teaching. It is quite fitting that one who appreciates words so dearly should also possess a way of making them feel meaningful.

I especially admire that thou puttest God first, and that even in thy kindness, thou remindest those around thee to take care of themselves, remain focused and keep moving forward.

May this new year of thy life bring thee closer to all that thou prayest for. May God guide thy steps, strengthen thy faith, reward thy efforts and grant thee beautiful reasons to smile.

Never lose the qualities that make thee uniquely thee. The world needs more of that.

Happy Birthday, Charity. May thy story continue to be a beautiful one.

With warmest wishes,
Thy humble knight.
